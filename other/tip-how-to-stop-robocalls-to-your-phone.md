### **TIP: How To Stop Robocalls To Your Phone**

### **Hi Guys,**

we have been asked quite a lot about how to stop Robocalls. There is not a foolproof solution to eliminate them, but there are a few things that you can do to minimise the risk…

### **WHAT ARE ROBOCALLS ?**

Robocalls are automated telephone calls that are made using a computerized system, often for the purpose of delivering pre-recorded messages or attempting to sell a product or service. These calls can be made in large numbers, and often target specific demographics or geographic areas.

Robocalls can be used for a variety of purposes, including political campaigns, telemarketing, debt collection, and scams. In some cases, robocalls can be illegal, such as when they are made to individuals on the National Do Not Call Registry or when they use deceptive tactics to try to trick people into providing personal information or money.

Robocalls have become increasingly prevalent in recent years, with some estimates suggesting that billions of robocalls are made each month in the United States alone. Many people find these calls to be disruptive and annoying, and there are a variety of tools and services available to help individuals block or avoid robocalls.

### There are several methods you can use to stop robocalls on your phone. Here are a few:

1. Register your phone number with the National Do Not Call Registry: The National Do Not Call Registry is a list of phone numbers that telemarketers are not allowed to call. You can register your phone number for free on the registry’s website at [www.donotcall.gov](http://www.donotcall.gov).
2. Use call-blocking apps: There are many apps available for both Android and iOS devices that can help block robocalls. Some popular options include Truecaller, Hiya, and RoboKiller.
3. Enable your phone’s built-in call-blocking feature: Many smartphones have built-in call-blocking features that can be enabled to block known robocallers or other unwanted calls. Check your phone’s settings or contact your service provider to see if this option is available.
4. Be cautious with your personal information: Robocallers often obtain phone numbers from public directories or other sources, but they can also use information you provide online or over the phone to target you with calls. Be cautious about providing your phone number to companies or websites, and never give out personal information to callers you don’t know or trust.
5. Hang up and report unwanted calls: If you receive a robocall, hang up immediately and report the call to the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) at [www.ftc.gov/complaint](http://www.ftc.gov/complaint). This helps the FTC track down and take action against illegal robocallers.
